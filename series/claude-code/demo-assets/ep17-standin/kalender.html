Move Calendar

A loose timeline for the relocation. Months are relative to “decision month” (M0), so the plan stays flexible while paperwork settles.

Calendar Area Research

Phase 1 — Decide & explore

  1. M0 Agree the family is serious. Set a rough budget range and a “go / no-go” checkpoint.
  2. M0 Shortlist 3–4 neighborhoods to research at a high level.
  3. M+1 Book a scouting trip (off-season if possible).

Phase 2 — Paperwork & logistics

  1. M+2 Read up on the relevant residence/visa category and required documents.
  2. M+2 Make a document folder: passports, insurance, school records.
  3. M+3 Get quotes for shipping vs. selling furniture.
  4. M+3 Compare health insurance options for the first months.

Phase 3 — Housing

  1. M+4 Arrange a short-term rental for the landing period.
  2. M+4 Walk the prepurchase / prerental checklist before signing anything.
  3. M+5 Confirm long-term housing only after living in the area for a few weeks.

Phase 4 — The move

  1. M+5 Final declutter; pack the “first 30 days” boxes separately.
  2. M+6 Travel out. Keep essential documents in carry-on.
  3. M+6 Register locally and set up basics (bank, SIM, school).

Later — Settle in

Review after the first season: is this working for the family? Re-open the go / no-go question honestly before committing to anything long-term.
