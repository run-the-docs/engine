# Gran Canaria — Prepurchase / Prerental Checklist

A generic checklist to run **before** committing to any rental or purchase. The goal is to slow ourselves down and check the boring things before signing anything.

## Before viewing
- [ ] Confirm the area matches our shortlist from the research notes
- [ ] Decide our hard limits (size, rooms, must-haves) ahead of time
- [ ] Have a translation app or a Spanish-speaking helper ready

## The property itself
- [ ] Visit at least twice, including once mid-week and once in the evening
- [ ] Check water pressure and that hot water actually works
- [ ] Test the internet connection in person, not on paper
- [ ] Look for damp, mold, and signs of past water damage
- [ ] Check windows, locks, and any shutters/blinds
- [ ] Note natural light at different times of day
- [ ] Ask about heating/cooling and typical comfort in summer

## The neighborhood
- [ ] Walk to the nearest shops, pharmacy, and transit stop
- [ ] Check noise levels (traffic, nightlife, construction)
- [ ] Confirm distance to the school options we care about
- [ ] Note parking reality if we’ll have a car

## Paperwork & process
- [ ] Understand exactly what documents we need before we can sign
- [ ] Get every promise in writing, not verbally
- [ ] Read the contract fully (translated) before agreeing to anything
- [ ] Know the cancellation / deposit terms
- [ ] Keep copies of everything in the document folder

## Sanity check
- [ ] Sleep on it. No same-day signatures.
- [ ] Re-open the go / no-go question honestly before committing
