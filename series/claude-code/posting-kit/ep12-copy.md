## LinkedIn (upload the 4:5 natively — no link in the body)

Closed your terminal and lost the whole conversation with Claude?

You didn't. Claude Code saves every session to disk as you work.

`claude --continue` picks the most recent one back up, right where you left off. `claude --resume` opens a picker of all your past sessions. Name one with `-n` and resume it by name later.

Already inside a session? `/resume` switches you to another.

Even after `/clear`, the old conversation is still there.
